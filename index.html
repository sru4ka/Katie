Questions

Before you apply

Do I still get a say in who I meet?

Always. We send you the shortlist and you choose who you'd like to meet. Nothing is booked without your approval, and your profile doesn't go live until you've signed off on it.

Which dating apps do you work with?

We work on the major dating apps. Essential covers one platform, Executive covers up to three, and Private Elite adds Instagram and personal brand management. Platform subscriptions are paid separately by you.

How do you keep this confidential?

Privacy is the default. We communicate through a secure client channel, never share your details without approval, and every profile we build is truthful and represents you as you really are.

How much of my time does this take?

A survey and one strategy call at the start, then a few minutes a week to review your matches and approve plans. The rest of your time goes to the dates themselves.

How will I know what's happening?

You get regular updates from Katie with your new matches, active conversations and upcoming dates, plus a weekly progress report.

I travel a lot. Can you help with that?

Yes. Executive includes travel dating preparation, and Private Elite adds advanced travel and date coordination so you can meet people wherever you are that week.
